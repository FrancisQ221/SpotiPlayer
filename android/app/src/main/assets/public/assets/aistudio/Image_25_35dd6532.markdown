## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered for an immersive desktop streaming and audio community platform. It fuses high-fidelity digital audio workflows with modern dark glassmorphism. The visual atmosphere evokes a midnight studio session: deep oceanic obsidian voids layered under crystalline, frosted-glass acrylic surfaces, punctuated by energetic bioluminescent accents.

### Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Atmospheric & Immersive:** Dark layered depths reduce optical fatigue during extended listening and curation sessions.
- **Precision & High Fidelity:** Crisp translucent panels, luminous meters, and refined typographic contrast signal studio-grade audio fidelity.
- **Electric Community Connection:** Vibrancy shifts intentionally from listening cues to patron appreciation, signaling communal support and artist patronage through warm solar energy alongside synthetic club neons.

### Visual Style Movement
A synthesis of **Dark Glassmorphism** and **Refined Cyber-Minimalism**. UI elements float above a multi-tiered abyss using backdrop blurs, delicate specular micro-borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`), subtle gradient underglows, and fluid pill-shaped controls.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-deep spectral hierarchy where dark surfaces recede into the backdrop, allowing glowing neon indicators and patron accents to take visual precedence.

### Canvas & Surfaces
- **Void Canvas (`#070B0E`):** Root background, global application shell, and deep underlays.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (`#0D1319`):** Persistent structural containers (side navigation, sticky bottom persistent player, auxiliary panels).
- **Surface Elevation 2 (`#131B22`):** Elevated cards, playlist drawers, and contextual dialogue overlays.
- **Glass Tint Layer:** `rgba(19, 27, 34, 0.65)` to `rgba(19, 27, 34, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`.

### Chromatic Accents
- **Neon Emerald Green (`#00E599`, `#10B981`):** Primary interaction anchor. Governs global playback controls, active equalizer visualizations, track progression bars, and currently active queue items.
- **Atmospheric Cyan (`#06B6D4`):** Secondary informational accent. Applied to genre badges, audio format badges (Hi-Res Lossless, Spatial Audio), collaborative playlist tags, and secondary action links.
- **Warm Amber Gold (`#F59E0B`, `#FBBF24`):** Tertiary community anchor. Dedicated exclusively to creator tips, micro-donations, VIP tier badges, patron heartbeats, and community spark metrics.
- **Specular Borders & Dividers:** `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` baseline, scaling up to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.16)` on active hover states.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its geometric precision, rounded warmth, and exceptional legibility across dense tracklists and dynamic metadata.

### Multilingual Support (ES / EN)
- All typographic lockups incorporate 25% horizontal headroom to accommodate text expansion between English and Spanish (e.g., "Library" vs. "Biblioteca", "Now Playing" vs. "Reproduciendo").
- Label styles maintain generous line-heights and moderate tracking to prevent optical crowding with Spanish diacritics (`á`, `é`, `í`, `ó`, `ú`, `ñ`, `ü`).
- Numerical counters, timestamp scrubbers, and audio bitrates preserve tabular figures (`tnum`) for layout stability during live playback.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical desktop fluid application framework with persistent anchors:

### Desktop Layout Architecture
- **Left Navigation Drawer:** Fixed width of 260px; houses root navigation, user playlists, and patron subscriptions.
- **Main Content Stage:** Fluid multi-column area utilizing an internal 12-column responsive layout with 24px (1.5rem) to 32px (2rem) gutters.
- **Right Contextual Deck (Collapsible):** 320px fixed rail for live synchronized lyrics, community patron feed, and queue details.
- **Bottom Persistent Player:** Fixed 96px docked glass shelf running full-viewport across the bottom edge.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop Large (1440px+):** Full 3-column stage (Navigation + Main Grid + Queue/Lyrics Deck).
- **Desktop Standard (1024px – 1439px):** Contextual deck docks into an overlay flyout; main grid expands across available canvas.
- **Compact Desktop / Tablet Landscape (768px – 1023px):** Side navigation collapses into an iconified rail (72px); bottom player condenses scrubber ergonomics.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered frosted glass and luminous environmental reflections rather than heavy physical dropshadows.

### Layer Stack
1. **Backdrop Void (Base 0):** Solid `#070B0E`. Houses ambient blur nodes (diffuse radial green and amber glows positioned beneath featured artist banners).
2. **Structural Glass (Tier 1):** `#0D1319` with 70% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`, bounded by a 1px solid stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
3. **Interactive Cards & Modals (Tier 2):** `#131B22` with 80% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, bordered with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, casting an ambient shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
4. **Active Focus & Hover Luminescence:** On mouse-over, primary interactive cards gain a targeted specular rim highlight (`rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.3)`) and a delicate radial drop glow: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.2)`. Patron-interactive elements shift this glow to warm amber: `0 0 24px -4px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`.

## Shapes

The design system embraces high curvature and organic fluid contours (Level 3 pill-shaped system), counterbalancing the deep dark technical canvas.

### Radius System
- **Full Pill (`9999px`):** Play/pause buttons, patron donate triggers, category chips, search inputs, volume handles, and badge containers.
- **Generous Container (`rounded-xl` / `24px`):** Album artwork wrappers, elevated glass cards, lyric modals, and contextual panels.
- **Nested Controls (`rounded-lg` / `16px`):** Internal cell highlights, playlist table row active states, and dropdown menus.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Play Action:** Full pill button with solid `#00E599` fill, `#070B0E` typography, font-weight 700. Hover triggers transform scale (1.04) and a neon aura `box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.45)`.
- **Secondary Glass Control:** Frosted translucent pill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)`), 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`, white text. Hover transitions background to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- **Patron & Tip Button:** Full pill button utilizing a radial gradient fill from `#FBBF24` to `#F59E0B`, paired with dark obsidian text and a leading micro-sparkle/heartbeat icon. Hover introduces dynamic pulse illumination: `0 0 20px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.5)`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Audio Spec Tags (Hi-Fi / Spatial):** Cyan tint (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)` background, `#06B6D4` text, 1px border `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.28)`), typography `label-sm`, uppercase with 0.06em tracking.
- **Patron & Supporter Badges:** Amber gold glass surface (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.15)`), `#FBBF24` label, accompanied by an inline heartbeat SVG spark.

### Track Lists & Table Rows
- **List Items:** Minimal height 56px with a base transparent surface.
- **Hover State:** Background transforms into `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)` with rounded 12px corners.
- **Active / Currently Playing State:** Frosted fill `rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.08)`, text shifted to `#00E599`, showing an animated 3-bar emerald frequency equalizer icon in place of track number.

### Input Fields
- **Global Search:** Full pill shell with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)` fill, 1px border `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, and blurred background. Focus states ignite a 1px border of `#00E599` alongside an inner shadow ring `0 0 12px rgba(0, 229, 153, 0.2)`.

### Cards (Album, Artist, Playlist)
- **Album / Show Card:** Frosted `#131B22` shell with 80% opacity, 16px padding, 20px rounded outer corner. Artwork inside features a 12px border radius. Floating green play button appears via translateY(-4px) and opacity fade-in over the artwork on hover.

### Audio Scrubbers & Volume Sliders
- **Track Rail:** 4px thickness, background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`. Active elapsed length rendered in `#00E599`. Scrub head is an 11px circular neon pill with glowing bloom, expanding to 14px during active drag.
